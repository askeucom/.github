<h3 align="center">AI that answers from EU law, not from memory.</h3>

AskEU is an AI guide that explains healthcare rights in the European Union in plain words. A person describes their situation, and the AI finds the rules that apply, writes the answer and links every point to an official source. If no checked rule applies, it says so instead of guessing.

AskEU is in development. Early access is planned for 2027, free for individuals, in English, German, Polish and Ukrainian.

### What the AI does

- Tells people where to go, what they are entitled to, what it costs and how to get their money back
- Answers only from a library of checked rules based on official EU and national sources
- Links every point of an answer to its official source and shows when it was last checked
- Says "I don't know" and names who to ask when no checked rule applies
- Tells people they are talking to an AI and labels every answer as AI-generated

### What the AI does not do

- Diagnose an illness or recommend treatment
- Answer without an official source or guess when it doesn't know
- Ask for or keep medical records
- Promote insurers or clinics for money
- Make decisions about anyone: reimbursement decisions are made by insurers and national authorities
