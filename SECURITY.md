# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in any AskEU repository or on ask.eu.com, please email **hello@ask.eu.com** with the subject **Security**.

Please include:

- what you found and where (repository, file or page)
- steps to reproduce it
- what impact you think it has

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion about a vulnerability, and do not share it publicly until we have answered and fixed it.

## What happens next

- A person reads every report.
- We confirm that we received your report within two working days.
- We tell you what we plan to do and keep you informed until the issue is fixed.
- If you wish, we credit you once the fix is public.

## Scope

AskEU never asks for health data, diagnoses or medical records. If you come across personal data while testing, do not copy or keep it, and tell us in your report.
