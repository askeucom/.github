# Code of conduct

AskEU helps people understand their healthcare rights in the EU. We want every space around the project, including issues, pull requests and messages, to be respectful and safe.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient. People write in different languages and with different levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas, not people. No insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Stay on topic and assume good intent.

## Personal and medical data

- Never post real personal or medical data: no names of patients, diagnoses, medical records, insurance numbers or identity documents, yours or anyone else's.
- If you describe a situation, make it general or fictional.
- Content with personal or medical data will be removed.

## Emergencies

AskEU and its repositories are not an emergency service. **In a medical emergency call 112**, the EU-wide emergency number.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email **hello@ask.eu.com**. A person reads every report, and we treat reports confidentially. We may remove content, warn or block anyone who breaks these rules.
